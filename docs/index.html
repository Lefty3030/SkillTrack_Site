Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

SkillTrack is a private, on-device app. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No account. SkillTrack has no login or user account.
  • No servers. Everything you enter stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app does not connect to the internet.
  • No analytics or tracking. SkillTrack uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • Your data. The skills, attendance, notes, and other information you record are stored only on your device. You can create an encrypted backup that you control, and erase all data at any time from within the app.
  • Patient information. SkillTrack is for clinical education and intentionally provides no field for patient-identifying information.

Because SkillTrack does not collect data, there is nothing for us to access, sell, or disclose.

Help & contact

Support

SkillTrack is a private, on-device logbook for nursing clinical practice. Open the app, accept the privacy notice, and choose your role - Student or Instructor. Your data stays on your device.

Common questions

  • Change role. Go to Settings → Erase all data to return to the role picker.
  • Back up or restore. On the Reports tab, use the backup icon to create a passphrase-encrypted backup you can restore later.
  • Works offline. SkillTrack never needs a connection - nothing is uploaded.
